# Reading tools

Select a word or passage in a note to see **Pin**, **Copy for discussion**, and **Find in notes**. Existing Markdown files work without additional markup.

## Return to reading

Following an internal reference or a link in the table of contents saves your original reading position. **Return to reading**, at the bottom left, takes you back even after several reference jumps. The × button discards that position.

Opening **Find in notes** also keeps your place. Returning to the graph preserves its course selection, node positions, and zoom within the same browser tab. Reading positions and graph views are stored in the current tab's session; they do not sync between devices.

## Pin a reference

Select a passage and choose **Pin**. For a displayed equation, hover or tap it and choose **Pin formula**. Keyboard users can focus an equation and press **Alt+P**.

The reference stays visible while the main text scrolls and while you follow links. Up to three references can be pinned; adding another replaces the oldest. Collapse the panel using its heading, or remove individual references with ×. Pins are temporary and clear on a page reload.

## Copy for discussion

Select a passage and choose **Copy for discussion**. The preview contains your selection, its section title, and a source link. Mathematical expressions retain their LaTeX.

Check **Include the whole current section** when the surrounding definitions or assumptions matter. Choose **Copy text**, then paste into your preferred conversation. This action copies text locally; it does not send a message.

## Find in notes

Select a term and choose **Find in notes** to search the note collection. Results include a short excerpt and link to the matching section. Edit the query to try a different phrase.

When the term exactly matches a registered concept name or alias, its other registered names are searched too. The extra names are shown above the results. These are text matches, not newly inferred graph relationships. Writing guides are excluded from this search; the main navigation search still covers the full site.
